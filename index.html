Record Merge and download Script How it works

Record the parts you're in, then tap Send my clip on each one to save the clip file and send it however your group shares files (text, email, Google Drive, Teams). Clips stay on this device until you delete them. Parts that already have a clip show Record my lines: your take is saved next to it, goes into the video you build, and never replaces anyone's clip.

For whoever puts the final video together. Add the clip files your partners sent you; each one goes to its part automatically from its file name. Then build: the matching PowerPoint slide fills the screen during presentation parts, with the speaker in the corner. Building plays the whole run once in real time, so keep this screen open until it finishes (about 10 minutes). Chrome or Edge on a laptop is the most reliable place to do this.

You can build any time, even with parts missing: missing parts are skipped and the progress bar shows which part is playing. Building never locks anything, so keep recording or adding parts and build again for an updated video.

    How the group uses this

    1. Everyone opens this same link on their own phone, tablet or laptop. No account or sign-in.
    2. Type all three names at the top of the Record tab.
    3. Record each part you're in. Your lines and, for presentation parts, your slide show beside the camera. Tap Next slide as you move through the slides.
    4. Watch the take back. Keep it, or delete it and record again.
    5. Tap Send my clip on each finished part. The clip file saves to your device (on iPhone, choose Save to Files or share it straight to a text or email). Send the files to whoever is merging.
    6. The person merging opens Merge and download, taps Add clips from your group, picks all the files, and builds the full video.

    Keep the file names the app gives the clips: the name tells the merge which part it is and when each slide changes. Clips live only on the device that recorded or added them, so nothing is uploaded anywhere.
